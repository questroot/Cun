A single frame of a pixel-art combat VFX: four flattened fog ellipses at staggered heights, each with a bright top edge.
Redesign brief:
Give the fog banks depth and layering: stacked overlapping fog masses with a clear bright top edge and a soft dark underside, so the bank reads as volume rather than a flat grey smudge.
Composition:
A small self-contained looping particle, filling roughly 40 to 50 percent of the cell, designed to repeat across the screen as ambient weather rather than to stand alone. At least 2 pixels of clear margin on all four sides.
One frame of an eight-frame seamless loop; the first and last frame must connect.
It is an ambient tile, not a one-off blast: keep the body compact so dozens on screen still read.
Silhouette rules:
One dominant mass must read first. No scattered debris, no hollow rings with an empty interior,
no floating fragments that do not connect to the main shape, no thin lines carrying the whole read.
Style/medium:
Premium hand-crafted 16-bit / 32-bit JRPG pixel VFX, crisp hard-edged square pixel clusters,
no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth gradients, no painted edges, no feathered glow.
Self-illuminated energy: bright core clearly separated from cool dark edges,
selective dark outlines only where they separate overlapping shapes.
Pixel density and tonal-step count must match the style anchor exactly.
Palette, exactly seven colours maximum: deep violet #302c4e, dark violet #514667, grey-violet #7a7291, pale lilac #afa1bd, pink-white #d8c9d8, near-white #f1e3ed, blue-grey #7a9fb2.
Dark base plus mid tones plus one bright accent, no extra hues.
Warm top-left light on solid props; self-luminous energy for glows, sparks and cores.
Background: solid perfectly flat bright green #00ff00 for clean keying,
no ground, no shadow, no checkerboard, no baked glow fog outside the effect.
Authentic game VFX asset, not vector art, not a flat geometric diagram,
