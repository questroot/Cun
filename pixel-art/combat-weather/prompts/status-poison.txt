Widen and thicken the pool so the poison reads as a substantial bubbling mass, not a thin flat smear: heavier stacked pool body, larger and denser rising bubbles, denser drifting haze. Keep it clearly distinct from the fog bank in shape.
Composition:
The effect alone, no character, no creature body, no wielder, no weapon held by anyone,

Centred on the effect anchor, filling roughly 70 to 80 percent of the cell,
at least 2 pixels of clear margin on all four sides.
One frame of an eight-frame seamless loop; the first and last frame must connect.

Silhouette rules:
One dominant mass must read first. No scattered debris, no hollow rings with an empty interior,
no floating fragments that do not connect to the main shape, no thin lines carrying the whole read.

no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth gradients, no painted edges, no feathered glow.

selective dark outlines only where they separate overlapping shapes.
